WADAMESH

by meshcomod

The whole mesh, on the radio itself.

MeshCore firmware with chat, channels, contacts, a map and apps on the device itself, by touch, keyboard or trackball, and a companion radio for the MeshCore app at the same time. Runs on 13 boards, from the LilyGo T-Deck and Heltec V4 to the Tanmatsu.

Two ways to install

Pick the path that fits how you like to use your device. You can switch later.

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. The firmware goes onto the board over a USB cable, and only those browsers let a web page do that. Open this page there:
wadamesh.com/#install
On a Tanmatsu, install WADAMESH from its app store instead.

Standalone

Just WADAMESH: the device boots straight into it.

Best if you want the simplest path: flash once, from your browser, and you're done.

With Launcher

Run WADAMESH as one app inside bmorcelli's Launcher on the LilyGo T-Deck.

Best if you want to keep multiple firmwares and switch between them from a menu. Every other board installs Standalone.

Standalone install

Plug the device in over USB and flash it right here. Works in Chrome or Edge on desktop.

Stable is the tested default. Pick it unless you want to help test. Beta · testing gets new features and fixes earlier, before they are proven, so it can be rough. It is for trying things early and reporting bugs.
Already running WADAMESH? You do not have to reflash to switch channels: turn on Settings → About → "Get test builds (beta)" on the device and it updates to test builds over Wi-Fi. Switch it off to go back to stable.
Testing a beta? Report from the device (Settings → About → "Send a test report") and it shows up on the test matrix, which is what the next stable is promoted on.

Beta builds are newer and less tested: for trying things early and reporting bugs. On a T-Deck under Launcher you download the .bin and side-load it.

    Questions

    Is it really free?
    Yes. WADAMESH is open source under GPL-3.0: no licence key and nothing locked. The code is on GitHub.
    Does it work with the MeshCore app?
    Yes. The device can be the radio for the official MeshCore app on your phone (iOS and Android) over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or USB, while you keep using it on its own. On the T-Display P4, Bluetooth pairing isn't available yet and on the Tanmatsu it has known gaps, so use Wi-Fi or USB there.
    Will I keep my identity, contacts and channels?
    Updating from the device keeps everything. A fresh install from this page can erase what is on the board, so make a backup first: Settings → Backups → Export backup saves your whole setup, including your node's identity, to the SD card when one is in. Without a card it stays on the board, so copy it off first with USB Files. Restore on the same page brings it back.
    How do I update later?
    From the device. When a new build is out, Settings → About shows it and Install update flashes it over Wi-Fi, no computer needed. A T-Deck under Launcher updates through Launcher, and the Tanmatsu through its app store.
    Stable or Beta?
    Stable is the tested build and the default. Beta gets new features and fixes first and can be rough: it is for trying things early and reporting bugs. Switch any time on the device with Settings → About → Get test builds (beta).
    Why can't I install from my phone?
    The firmware goes onto the board over a USB cable, and only Chrome and Edge on a computer let a web page do that. Open wadamesh.com on a computer. A Tanmatsu installs WADAMESH from its own app store.
    My board isn't detected
    Try another cable first: many USB cables only charge. Close anything else using the port, such as a serial monitor or another tab, then put the board in download mode. On a T-Deck, hold the trackball while you tap reset.
    Can I go back to the original firmware?
    Yes, nothing is locked. Flash stock MeshCore the same way from the official MeshCore web flasher, or your board maker's own firmware.
    Where do I get help?
    Ask on Discord, read the user guide, or open an issue with your board and what you did.
