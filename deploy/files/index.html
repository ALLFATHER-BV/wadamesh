WADAMESH FILES

The SD card and internal storage of your device, over a USB cable.

This page needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. Your browser cannot talk to a USB serial device (no Web Serial).
  1. On the device, open USB Files in the app drawer.
  2. Connect it to this computer with a USB cable.
  3. Click Connect and choose the device.
