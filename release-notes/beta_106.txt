# beta_106 - community round: web console with on-device approval, map traffic pulses, pictures in Lua apps, city locator, Bulgarian Bluetooth keyboard, Tanmatsu standalone build, Texas Hold'em, battery and touch fixes. TEST BUILD.
# One user-facing note per non-blank, non-# line; # lines are section comments.
# Order: what this build is, new, fixes, boards, apps.

Test build, on top of beta_105. The stable build stays beta_101.

# --- New ---
Web console: the browser pages on the Wi-Fi address are one console now, with Mesh, Screen, Files, Settings and Dashboard tabs and settings for your Lua apps. A new browser is let in by tapping Allow on the device instead of typing a code, and the card shows its IP address. Sensitive actions from the browser ask on the device first, and the Screen tab cannot answer those questions itself. On the T-LoRa Pager the Settings and Dashboard tabs are left out for space (#857 by oumike).
Map: short pulses show traffic while the Map tab is open. A message you send to a contact runs blue along its line, a message received runs green and an ACK yellow back to you; channel traffic spreads as a ring from your own marker. Mute colours and animations turns them off (#859 by oumike, #827). Not on the T-LoRa Pager.
Lua apps can draw pictures (PNG, JPEG or BMP) from their own files with wada.ui.image (#865 by CarpeNoctem). Not on the T-LoRa Pager.
City locator: with a cities.bin boundary file on the SD card, crossing into a new US city or town shows "Now in <city>". Settings, GPS, City on Home shows the city on Home instead of the node name; it is off by default. Nothing is transmitted (#858 by Languskhan).
Bluetooth keyboards type Bulgarian (phonetic) while the keyboard language is Bulgarian, and Ctrl+Shift switches the keyboard language like the language key. The layout printed on the keys stays what you picked (#855 by kkazakov). Not on the T-LoRa Pager.

# --- Fixes ---
The EN/BG keyboard tag in a chat no longer covers the GPS icon (#860 by kkazakov).
Cyrillic text shows in settings fields instead of empty boxes (#864 by kkazakov).
Battery calibration refuses to run while USB is plugged in: the charger's voltage was stored as "full", so a full battery then read about 60%. A calibration taken that way is ignored and the default curve returns (reported by sonicblue7 and rausimous on the T-Deck Pro).

# --- Boards ---
Tanmatsu: a standalone image that replaces the launcher can be installed from wadamesh.com (beta), next to the app store install. Your identity, contacts and chats are kept; settings start fresh (#809 by oumike, #806).
Tanmatsu: Enter in a chat sends the message, arrows leave the text box at its ends, Enter picks options in the setup wizard, the Wi-Fi scan waits for the radio to finish, F1 and F4 only wake a dark screen, and a radio preset no longer turns back into Custom (#809 by oumike).
Tanmatsu: larger tab icons with the F-key shape beside them, F-key hints beside the chat buttons, and a two-column repeater admin layout (#862 by oumike).
CrowPanel 3.5: touch works after a restart from Settings. The touch chip can come back on its second address, and the 3.5 now follows it and resets it if it does not answer (reported by kc9seb).
ThinkNode M9: on USB the screensaver and power saving stay on USB behaviour after the charger stops at full (reported by benanderson).

# --- Apps ---
New in the Store: Texas Hold'em, no-limit poker against three AI opponents, in portrait and landscape (#856 by halflive-743).
Cyberspace 1.1 in the Store: email, a one-time password and Stay signed in, set from the web console, which now has a settings page for every app that declares them (#857 by oumike).
Language store v34: the new strings of this round in all 15 languages.
