# beta_100 - Pager hardware (vibration, fuel gauge, ship mode, motion), PMTiles maps, contact auto-delete, GPS Auto speed, many fixes. TEST BUILD.
# One user-facing note per non-blank, non-# line; # lines are section comments.
# Order: what this build is, new, fixes, boards, apps.

Test build, on top of beta_99. The stable build stays beta_85.

# --- New ---
Maps from one file: put a raster PMTiles archive in /maps on the SD card and pick it under Map options. Vector and JPEG archives are refused with the reason in the picker, and "Zoom: map levels only" keeps the map inside the levels the file has (#759 by kremers).
Contacts can delete themselves when not heard from in a chosen number of days (Settings, Contact Management, off by default). Favourites and the chat that is open are never deleted, and nothing goes while the clock is not set (#731 by oumike, asked for in #719).
Contacts has a Rooms filter (#740 by oumike, asked for by green.six).
Backups are listed by their date, or "Default", instead of the file name (#741 by oumike), and on the P4 boards the date follows your time zone after a Wi-Fi sync (#756 by oumike).
GPS speed: the T-Deck and the ThinkNode M9 find their GPS's serial speed by themselves. Settings, GPS, Serial speed has a new Auto choice, the default; a speed you pick is used as it is.
Screensaver: "Keep north up" stops the slow turn of the picture, so north stays at the top (#706, asked for by i-TomCZ).
Lua apps: wada.ui.input takes an optional 4th argument, the longest text it accepts (up to 2000 characters, 63 without it), and caps().input_len says the device has it (asked for by rock.paper.scissors_).
Polish (Polski) is new in the Store's languages, translated by tom73god.

# --- Fixes ---
A repeater's status, neighbours and access list stayed empty in the admin app after a telemetry poll or a Home sensor widget asked a node that never answered: that request held the device's single request slot until a reboot. It now lets go after 45 seconds (reported by enthusiastric).
Updating from the device picks a dropped or stalled download up where it stopped, up to 3 times, instead of failing with "short read" (#689, reported by Hixsten).
Import backup showed an empty page with two or more backups, and the time zone and Wi-Fi scan pages could open blank: the page was measured before it was laid out (#738 by oumike; #757, #760, reported by Ender436 and Kb1hux).
Wi-Fi after a lost link: the device's retry no longer races the Wi-Fi chip's own reconnect and no longer clears the saved network, it slows down after a minute so an absent network is not searched all night, and waking the screen tries once at once (#685, reported by william-stearns and stephen-62).
Wi-Fi switched on from the Home widget or the control center joins straight away, not only after opening Settings, Wi-Fi.
Time zone: Settings, Clock & time keeps the "By hand" field in the zone you just picked, so Set clock after a zone change no longer moves the clock by the difference, and the CrowPanel P4 10.1 no longer resets the zone to UTC on a Wi-Fi time sync (#699, reported by zomgugoff).
Avatars never go blank: a name starting with a flag or joined emoji took an invisible character as its icon, and Initials now skip emoji and take whole letters, so "ãna" shows ÃN (#674, reported by neoemit).
T-Deck: an older keyboard that sent a burst like "QERUOXV$ ZNMFJ" for one Q is recognised, switched to the older keyboard setting and remembered.
T-Deck: with Italian, Spanish or Romanian selected, Sym+W/E/R type 1 2 3 again; the accented letters come from the accent box after the base letter (#747, reported by QuakeRadio).
Save update bin to SD downloads the file first and then writes it to the card, retrying the whole file, and the SD health check keeps off the card meanwhile (#734, reported by Jess-39).
A wallpaper in internal /lock/ survives a reboot: the start-up cleanup of old map tiles deleted every picture on internal storage.
Lua apps: wada.ui.input returns the text when Enter is pressed, also from an external keyboard on a touch board (the second Enter used to land on Cancel), wada.sys.random(n) gives 1 to n, random(1, 1) gives 1 instead of a huge number, and a random over the full number range no longer restarts the device.
Settings that fail to save to an SD slot file recreate the file and try again (#744 by oumike, #743).
A restore that cannot save identity, channels and contacts says so instead of restarting into an empty store (#708, reported by zomgugoff, built on #709 by oumike).

# --- Boards ---
T-LoRa Pager: the vibration motor buzzes for messages, with patterns and strength in Settings, Sound (#748 by MrYoshii).
T-LoRa Pager: the charger and fuel gauge show real charge, current and time to full or empty, with battery care (stop at 80%), a temperature guard and low-battery warnings that switch off safely at 3% (#749 by MrYoshii).
T-LoRa Pager: Power off is a real power-off (ship mode) when not on USB; hold the power key or plug in USB to start it again. Wi-Fi uses modem sleep while the screen is off (#750 by MrYoshii).
T-LoRa Pager: the motion sensor turns notifications quiet while the Pager lies face down, can wake the screen when you pick it up (opt-in), and Lua apps can read it (#751 by MrYoshii).
T-LoRa Pager: the lock screen can have a wallpaper (#752 by MrYoshii).
ThinkNode M9: a slow key press that flickers on release types one letter, not two; the same letter again is only taken after a moment that follows your typing pace (#671, reported by neoemit).
T-Display P4: vibrates on new messages with the AW86224 motor, off by default and quiet under Do Not Disturb (#730 by ItsNix).
T-Display P4: with the keyboard attached the battery percentage comes from the voltage, since the gauge is set up for the small internal cell (#722 by oumike, #721).
T-Display P4 under Launcher: an SD card Launcher left in SPI mode is power-cycled and mounted again at start-up (#708, built on #709 by oumike).
T-Deck Pro: Settings, the header and the lock screen fit the 240 px e-paper panel (#742 by oumike).
SenseCAP Indicator: the map says the board has no map storage instead of asking to reflash a partition it does not have (#692, reported by skykingisepic).
